Industry capstoneSep 2026 to now

Honeywell Aerospace: AeroSecure AIAn AI-powered aerospace maintenance platform, built for and with Honeywell

It predicts how long a replacement part will take to arrive, recommends which supplier to order from, and controls who can see restricted data. We're a team of 4 working in 2-week Scrum sprints, with weekly reviews from Honeywell engineers.

  • Built role-based access control for 20,000 order records (student, mentor, admin) with deny-by-default rules and an audit log of every request, served through FastAPI
  • Built the supplier recommendation engine that combines the model's delivery predictions with shipment restriction rules to recommend a supplier, escalate, or block an order
  • Wrote automated tests with pytest and unittest, validated against Honeywell's official policy and maintenance scenarios
  • Set up AWS (S3, IAM roles, SageMaker) for cloud model training, and data access tiers in S3
  • Help plan sprints in Jira and present them to Honeywell for approval

Stack Python, FastAPI, scikit-learn, pandas, AWS, pytest, Jira
